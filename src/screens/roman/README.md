# Roman chat

`RomanChatScreen.tsx` serves client and coach chat. It is built on the shared `src/ui` `Screen` (top inset, keyboard-aware footer) with `ScreenTopBar` (labelled 44 pt Back whenever mounted in a navigator, conversation-history action on the right), a serif `Headline` "Roman" and an overline ("AI assistant · Working with your coach", "AI assistant" for coachless clients, "AI assistant · For your practice" for coaches). Back calls the current stack's `goBack`, including loading, offline, unavailable and error states.

The room (ROMAN-ROOM-133, B27, B30, prototype 69-73): an empty client chat opens on Roman's whole portrait beside one serif line (time of day, the client's first name, what Roman can explain with their own numbers). Four quick-start chips sit above the composer on the client surface (Explain my targets, Today's workout, Hit my protein, How was my week); a chip sends its words as the client's message, never clears a typed draft, and a failed chip leaves its words in the composer. Roman's replies are serif reading text with no bubble (paragraphs and bullets from the plain reply); a reply that arrives after a send fades in paragraph by paragraph (240 ms, Reduce Motion shows it at once), history never animates. The client's turns are quiet right-aligned bubbles (`radius.card`). The composer field (`radius.input`) and the forest send square (`radius.button`) float in the `Screen` footer above the keyboard; the footer line reads "Workout and food guidance only. Not medical advice." The coach surface keeps its own greeting and has no chips or footer line.

Home's Roman shortcut navigates to `MoreTab / RomanChat` with `initial: false`, so a first visit keeps `MoreIndex` underneath chat. The You menu, settings, membership and sign-out remain reachable without restarting. The You-menu Roman row continues to use its existing stack navigation.

`useRomanChat.ts` reads the existing account-bound `GET /roman/sessions?limit=1` before opening today's session. Only an account with no earlier chats and an empty resumed session gets the first-meeting introduction; an earlier chat or failed history lookup selects returning-user copy. This is greeting metadata only: chat opening, sending, refusal handling, quotas, deletion events and paging are unchanged.

Regression evidence: `__tests__/RomanChatRoom133.test.tsx` covers the launch state, overlines, chips, reply blocks, the reveal under Reduce Motion and the room at 360x800 and 390x844 (snapshots). `../../components/roman/__tests__/RomanChatGuidance.test.tsx` covers turn and composer styling. `__tests__/RomanChatNav.test.tsx` covers Back in every load state, client/coach parity, history, composer editing, preserved draft, send retry and older messages. `__tests__/RomanGreetingHistory.test.tsx` covers no prior chats, returning clients and failed history reads. `../../components/home/__tests__/HomeHeaderActions.test.tsx` pins the nested-navigation payload and the unchanged message/bell actions.
